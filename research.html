RESEARCH

Research

Mathematical theory, modelling and applications.

Non-autonomous Dynamical Systems

Analysis of systems with time-dependent parameters, including persistence, stability, boundedness, periodicity and long-term dynamics.

Spatio-temporal Models

Reaction-diffusion and related partial differential equation models incorporating spatial structure and temporal variation.

Ecological Modelling

Mathematical investigation of interacting populations, environmental variability, dispersal and spatial pattern formation.

Epidemiological Modelling

Mathematical models of disease and virus propagation, including spatial spread, thresholds, persistence and intervention effects.

Mathematical Theory

Potential theoretical themes include existence and uniqueness, invariant regions, stability, persistence, attractors, bifurcation, travelling waves, pattern formation and qualitative properties of non-autonomous partial differential equation systems.
